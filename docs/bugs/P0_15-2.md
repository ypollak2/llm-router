## P0.15-2. First-run grandfathering loses compat models and mistakes hook history for upgrades

- **Symptom.** Upgrades omit detected OpenAI-compatible models when Ollama discovery claims first; fresh installs can grandfather a later pull after hook bookkeeping or an empty first chain.
- **Cause.** The one-time claimant receives only its caller's inventory, any usage or routing-decision row establishes history, and empty chains return before claiming.
- **Fix.** Collect compat IDs inside the upgrade claim with recorded fail-open handling; exclude cc usage, DIRECT hook decisions and the DIRECT usage twin (`reason='direct'`, written by `savings_logger.log_direct_to_db` with the routed provider), require usage history for decision schemas without reason_code, claim before the empty-chain return, and correct both docstrings.
- **Test.** tests/test_p015_grandfather_edges.py covers discover-first compat claims, hook-only usage, empty-chain markers, real routed usage, DIRECT decision filtering, a real `log_direct_to_db` route (red on the first revision, green after), and compat lookup failure.
