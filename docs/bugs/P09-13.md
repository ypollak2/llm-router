## P09-13. No kpi line reported the classifier-shadow scheduling cost after P09-11

- **Symptom.** The plan target "shadow <= 30 ms" (`PLAN.md` G1-proxy, line 230; not v16-ratified — PLAN-v16 has no separate shadow figure)
  had no reading in `llm-router kpi`. Review of #367 (merged b4e6a340) found it: the scheduling cost
  was added to `tier_decision_s` only, and since #367 G1_proxy turn-first measures the
  `DECISION_PHASES` sum, which excludes it. Two texts still said G1_proxy reads `tier_decision_s` on
  turn-first (the comment in `proxy/server.py` and the docstring of
  `test_the_scheduling_cost_is_inside_the_ledgered_decision_time`).
- **Cause.** P09-11 moved the figure to the phase sum (correct) and noted that the shadow cost left
  every segment, but did not give that cost a reading of its own.
- **Fix.** `decide_tier` times the `cls_shadow.maybe_schedule` call and writes
  `tier_shadow_schedule_ms` on the row only when a call was scheduled (not on off, skipped or
  dropped; no request content). `G1_proxy` adds a `shadow schedule` segment (p50 / p95, n,
  sessions, within/OVER the 30 ms target; `not informative` below n=100 or 2 sessions, same rule as
  P09-11). `tier_decision_s` is unchanged (still wall time with scheduling included; the
  continuation segment reads it). The two stale texts are corrected. `docs/repo_goals/KPIS.md` G1.
- **Rule.** When a measure is moved off a field, the thing that field also carried gets its own
  reading in the same change, or the gap is stated on the line.
- **Test.** `tests/test_kpi_command.py`:
  `test_g1_proxy_reports_the_shadow_scheduling_cost_against_the_30ms_target`,
  `test_g1_proxy_shadow_schedule_not_informative_cases_and_absent_when_shadow_off`;
  `tests/proxy/test_llm_classifier_shadow.py`:
  `test_the_schedule_ms_field_is_on_the_row_only_when_a_shadow_call_was_scheduled`,
  `test_the_schedule_ms_field_is_absent_with_the_shadow_off`. The first three fail on main (6d0b7535); the fourth (field absent with the shadow off) is a guard that passes there too.
