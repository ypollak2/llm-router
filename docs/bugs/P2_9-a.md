## P2.9-a. Three local agents, three write policies; `llm_local_task` took any workdir

- **Symptom.** `llm_act`, `llm_local_task` and the toolkit each had their own containment test and their own idea of a
  destructive command. `llm_local_task(workdir=...)` resolved no project root at all: a caller could point it at any directory.
  With `LLM_ROUTER_AGENT_COMMANDS=all` and `LLM_ROUTER_AGENT_WRITES=apply`, `rm -r`, `git push` and `git reset --hard` ran in the loop
  (only `rm -rf /`, `mkfs`, `dd if=` and `curl|sh` were caught).
- **Cause.** The checks were copied per lane (`react._resolve`, `agent_loop._resolve_path`, `toolkit.policy.resolve_in_workspace`)
  and the destructive table lived only in `agent_writes` allowlist mode, which `all` skips.
- **Fix.** `write_policy.contain` is the one containment test; `check_write`, `check_action` (shell tripwire + `DESTRUCTIVE` table, needs
  approval = refused while approval is unwired) and `pick_root` sit beside it. `llm_local_task` blocks a workdir outside a known
  root (MCP roots, then `CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR`); with no known root the workdir is the root. Static tripwire, not a sandbox.
- **Test.** `tests/test_write_policy_one_policy.py`: 10 outside-root + 10 destructive attempts per lane through the real executors,
  door tests for `llm_act`, `llm_local_task` and the toolkit, a spy proving all lanes use `contain`, and six mutants.
