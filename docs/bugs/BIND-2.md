## BIND-2. Codex ran read-only, so most delegated answers reported blocked work, and the router handed those over as the sub-agent's result

- **Symptom (2026-10-04..2026-11-03).** 314 of 317 delegated outputs mention read-only or sandbox, and 269 (85%) also say blocked, cannot or unable to (keyword screen over the delegated texts, n=317, `~/.rsi/research/primary-plan/v16/coord/BIND-R1-codex-handoff-20261103.md` section 2.8). A hand read of 9 found 8 that end "not performed", "needs a write-enabled session" or "read-only permissions prevented the worktree or tests". The prompts came from coordinator sessions that ask a sub-agent to clone, run tests, open a PR and write a report.
- **Cause.** `run_codex` passed no `--sandbox` flag (`src/llm_router/codex_agent.py`), and `codex exec` then runs read-only. `_is_codex_suitable` filters on task-type words and a multi-file-write phrase only. Nothing checked whether an answer reported work NOT done, so a "blocked" report was denied-through to Claude as if a sub-agent had finished.
- **Fix.** `run_codex(sandbox=...)` and `codex_exec_args()`: a delegated agent run gets `--sandbox workspace-write -c sandbox_workspace_write.network_access=false` (the key was checked with `codex debug prompt-input`, which reports "Network access is restricted" for false and "enabled" for true), in the session's directory (the hook payload's `cwd`, not the hook process's); `danger-full-access` cannot be requested through the function, and a filesystem-root `cwd` is refused (`codex_unsafe_cwd`). Pure-LLM callers pass no sandbox and their argv is unchanged. A reply that says the environment stopped the work (`_blocked_marker`: an environment phrase and an inability phrase in one sentence, or a conclusive phrase such as "Resume in a write-enabled session") is not delivered: the unit is logged `codex_blocked`, no saving is booked, and the Agent call is allowed. `northstar` counts `codex_blocked` as a discarded attempt so those runs stay in the denominator. Detector calibration on the delegated texts above (n=317, produced read-only, texts joined from `~/.rsi/research/primary-plan/v16/coord/BIND-R1-codex-handoff-20261103.md` section 2.1 data): 300 flagged; of the 211 whose end was captured, 207 flagged, and the 4 not flagged were finished reviews with a verdict. It has not been measured on writable-sandbox answers: after deploy, hand-check 20 bound units (success bar: under 10% blocked).
- **Test.** `tests/test_bind_agent_route_handoff.py::test_built_command_line_of_a_delegated_run` (the stub records the real argv and cwd), `::test_exec_args_workspace_write_has_the_network_off_and_precedes_the_model`, `::test_exec_args_default_is_unchanged_for_every_pure_llm_caller`, `::test_no_caller_can_ask_for_an_unsandboxed_run`, `::test_a_filesystem_root_is_never_the_writable_root`, `::test_a_blocked_reply_is_not_bound_and_the_agent_call_is_allowed`, `::test_blocked_reports_are_recognised` (10 phrasings), `::test_answers_that_mention_the_words_in_passing_are_not_blocked` (7 answers that must stay bound), and `tests/test_northstar.py::test_agent_route_codex_blocked_is_a_discarded_attempt`. Narrow mutations each turned a named test red: no network flag, no sandbox flag, no cwd, root not refused, blocked gate off, `codex_blocked` unmapped.
