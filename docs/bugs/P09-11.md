## P09-11. `kpi` G1_proxy turn-first p95 read 5 ms from rows that were not turn-first

- **Symptom.** An independent recompute from the raw proxy ledger (2026-10-09; its window and
  ledger copy are that recompute's, the figures were not re-measured in this change) found the P0.9-e
  turn-first decision figure in `llm-router kpi` at p95 5.0 ms over a bucket of about 2,771 rows
  with a null `step_class`, while the real turn-first population (`step_class == turn_first`,
  decision = sum of `tier_phases_ms` over `proxy.tiers.DECISION_PHASES`) gave about 54-70 ms.
  The PRD's 50 ms heuristic bar was read as met by a population that does not decide turns.
- **Cause.** `commands/kpi.py` `_g1_proxy` put every row that was not `continuation` and not
  `tier_reason == side_call` in the turn-first bucket. Null-step rows (side calls and pre-GE1
  rows with no step label, `proxy/ledger.py` "null = any other kind") and `subagent_first` rows
  landed there. The bucket was also measured as `tier_decision_s`, which is wall time including
  the classifier-shadow scheduling, not the P0.9-e phase sum. No n-per-session gate existed, so
  one session could carry the figure.
- **Fix.** The turn-first segment counts only `step_class == turn_first` rows that are not a side
  call by either label, measured as the `DECISION_PHASES` sum; a row without those phases is left
  out (never back-filled from `tier_decision_s`). The line prints n, sessions and the largest
  session's share, says `not informative` below n=100 or with fewer than 2 sessions (P0.9-e MUST),
  and counts the rows it left out (no step_class, subagent_first, turn_first without
  `tier_phases_ms`). The continuation segment is unchanged. Side effect: the classifier-shadow
  scheduling cost, which `proxy/server.py` adds to `tier_decision_s` only, is no longer in any
  G1_proxy segment (`docs/repo_goals/KPIS.md`, G1).
- **Rule.** A latency bucket is defined by the label it claims (`step_class == turn_first`), never
  by "not the other labels": a null label is not a kind.
- **Test.** `tests/test_kpi_command.py`:
  `test_g1_proxy_turn_first_counts_only_turn_first_rows_with_decision_phases` (fixture ledger with
  null-step, side_call, subagent_first, continuation and turn_first rows with and without
  `tier_phases_ms`; exact p50/p95, n and exclusion counts),
  `test_g1_proxy_null_step_rows_cannot_pull_the_turn_first_p95_down` (2,000 null-step rows at 5 ms
  beside 100 turn-first decisions at 60 ms) and
  `test_g1_proxy_turn_first_from_one_session_or_under_100_is_not_informative`. All three fail on
  7aaf8c96.
