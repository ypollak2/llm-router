The judge sampler no longer loses a lock-timed-out pick when a short-lived process exits: a pick that timed out on the 2 s state lock was counted only in memory, so `seen + lock_skipped` could fall one short of the calls (CI saw 399 of 400 once in `test_two_processes_lose_no_counts`, PR #967 run, 2026-11-25). The pending skips are now flushed at exit (one blocking attempt; a failed flush is recorded as `CHZ-FO-JUDGE-SAMPLER-LOCK`), a forked child does not re-flush its parent's skips, and both write paths use the private state writer.
