`llm-router local-check` item bank (LC-1, data and loader only; no command yet): `llm_router.local_check.bank` loads `data/bank-v1.jsonl.gz` and `data/manifest.json`, re-hashes the file against the manifest sha256 on every load and refuses on any mismatch, and exposes the 140 items (70 commit_message, 70 pr_description; strata size x language), two disjoint 70-item draws seeded by the bank sha, and 30 judge-validity plants (12 good, 12 swapped, 6 mutated) from a pool disjoint from every item. The text is public diffs and commit/PR messages from MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC or Unlicense projects; each record names repo, commit sha, SPDX licence and URL, and `data/NOTICE` carries the attribution. Pylint (GPL-2.0) is excluded. `scripts/build_local_check_bank.py` rebuilds the bank reproducibly and reads each licence from the LICENSE file at the record's sha. Evidence: `tests/test_local_check_bank.py` (tamper refused, schema, licence filter, draws, plant disjointness, privacy grep, 2 MB cap, wheel contents).
