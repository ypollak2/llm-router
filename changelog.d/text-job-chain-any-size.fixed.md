`llm_text_job` threw away local-model work in two ways, and all are closed. (1) The chain of a text job followed its input: with no `complexity_hint`, `route_and_call` derived the tier from the prompt, a prompt over 2000 characters read as `complex`, the PREMIUM chain injects no local model, and `prefer_passed` adds nothing, so with `LLM_ROUTER_LOCAL_TEXT_JOBS` unset the passed lane was refused (`lane_refused`, "not in the router's chain") and with `=1` the local model was not in the chain at all; under `LLM_ROUTER_ENGINE_MODE=decide` the engine's verdict on the wording did the same. `llm_text_job` now passes a fixed `moderate` tier, so the chain no longer depends on the size or the wording of the input. (2) The generic quality score (`score_response`, `LLM_ROUTER_ESCALATE_THRESHOLD` 0.4) rates a plain one-line commit message under that threshold, so with `=1` the local answer the job's own check would accept was rejected as `low_quality` and a pricier model was called; every such score was also recorded for `should_skip_model`, which after three of them skipped the local model (flag unset: the fourth job in one server process was handed back as `local_failed`). A text job's answer (`eligibility_exempt_reason == TEXT_JOB_REASON`, which only `llm_text_job` passes) is no longer scored, escalated or recorded and goes to `check_output`; every other call keeps the escalation unchanged. The lane guards are untouched: enabled, passed, digest-verified, settings-matched, inside `scope.max_prompt_chars`, loopback only, and a pass-only job still never reaches a cloud model. Regression tests: `tests/test_lc12c.py` (real chain builder at 300, 2000 and 40000 characters with the flag unset and `=1`, the engine verdict, scope and no-lane refusals, the escalation, the quality breaker); `docs/bugs/TJCHAIN-1.md`, `docs/bugs/TJCHAIN-2.md`. (3) The router's structural compaction cut a large text-job prompt to its first 20 and last 10 lines, so the model described a change it could not see; a text job is no longer compacted (TJCHAIN-3).
