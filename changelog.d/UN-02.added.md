`llm-router prefer` caps, "only local for <task>" and "no paid API" now steer routing at the MCP/router and hook doors and at the proxy's non-Claude model choice. A per-day cap ("cap codex N/day") drops that provider or model once the router has executed that many calls for it in the rolling 24 hours; a percent cap ("cap claude N%") drops it while its quota pressure on the resource map is at or above the percent. "only local for code" keeps only models that stay on this machine for that task type (other task types are unchanged; no local model means the usual no-route result). "no paid API" keeps local models, Codex, Gemini CLI and, in Claude subscription mode, Claude. All three apply to a pinned model too, and a cap that cannot be read never blocks routing. A cap on a Claude model at the proxy tier decision is not covered.
