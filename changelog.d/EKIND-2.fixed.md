Proxy: the unhealthy log line no longer hides the cause of common local failures behind `kind=other`. New fixed-vocabulary kinds `step_budget_exceeded`, `hedge_timeout`, `truncated` (cut off at num_predict), `unknown_tool` and `tool_args_invalid` are matched from the start of the error text and never copy it. They are not runner kinds: the runner answered, so they keep the plain cooldown. Other error texts still map to `other`.
