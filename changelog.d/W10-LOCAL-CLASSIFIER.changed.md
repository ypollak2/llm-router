`LLM_ROUTER_LOCAL_CLASSIFIER` unset now means `shadow` when the decision model is configured (`LLM_ROUTER_CLASSIFIER_BACKEND=systemone` and `LLM_ROUTER_DECISION_MODEL`), else `off`; an explicit `off` still turns it off. Measured on the real hook (2026-11-04, 20 model calls, load average 5.1-6.5): shadow adds p50 17 ms and p95 37 ms per prompt over a classifier-off baseline of 120 ms (n=6 warm, n=4 after an eviction: p95 53 ms), because a detached worker makes the call. With the classifier on, session start loads the decision model (`nimble:9b`) through its own endpoint in the detached child and skips the edit-model and generic warm-ups, which loaded a 22 GB model and evicted it (opt out with `LLM_ROUTER_CLASSIFIER_PRELOAD=off`; the zero-Claude edit model is no longer pinned at session start, and 0 of 571 real prompts were edit-eligible in 30 days). A prompt decided in the hook's own process is held at most `LLM_ROUTER_DOOR_VERDICT_PROMPT_BUDGET_MS` (2,000), and one that finds the model not resident does not wait for the load.
