Proxy: a local backend failure now carries a fixed-vocabulary kind (`backend_error_kind` on the ledger row, `backend_health.error_kind` on the breaker info, `kind=` in the unhealthy log line): `runner_oom`, `runner_compute_error`, `xml_parse`, `http_500`, `empty_body`, `other`. It is matched from the error text and never copies runner text. Without `LLM_ROUTER_BACKEND_RECYCLE_CMD`, a runner_oom / runner_compute_error trip stays unhealthy 300 s instead of probing every 60 s (the 2026-11-21 22:08-22:11Z Metal OOM produced ~115 instant `Compute error` failures); with it, the existing recycle (at most one per 10 min) still handles the reload.
