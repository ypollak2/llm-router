Proxy: a local backend failure now carries a fixed-vocabulary kind (`backend_error_kind` on the ledger row, `backend_health.error_kind` on the breaker info, `kind=` in the unhealthy log line): `runner_oom`, `runner_compute_error`, `runner_empty_burst`, `xml_parse`, `http_500`, `empty_body`, `other`. It is matched from the error text and never copies runner text. A runner kind keeps the backend unhealthy 300 s instead of 60 s when `LLM_ROUTER_BACKEND_RECYCLE_CMD` is unset; with it set, the existing recycle (at most one per 10 min) handles the reload. Runner errors reach the proxy as `runner_oom` / `runner_compute_error` only when the error text arrives (HTTP 500 body, `ollama error` line). Most of the 2026-11-21 22:08-22:11Z Metal OOM did not: 134 of 136 chats were HTTP 200 with an empty body. Three consecutive empty replies, each back in under 2 s within 10 s, now count as `runner_empty_burst` (same longer cooldown and recycle trigger). One empty reply, or empties that took normal prefill time, keep the plain 60 s cooldown.
