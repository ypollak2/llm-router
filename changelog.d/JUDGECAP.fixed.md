JUDGECAP: a judge queue at its entry cap whose size sat just under a 256 KiB check line was fully read and rewritten on every enqueue (each append crossed the line, each trim went back under it). A cap trim on a large queue now also drops enough of the oldest entries to leave a quarter stride (64 KiB) below the next line, so at the cap a full check needs at least that many appended bytes. Evidence: `test_queue_at_the_cap_just_below_a_stride_line_is_not_reread_per_enqueue` in `tests/test_p31_judge_hygiene.py` (N=400 enqueues of one entry size, cap set so the queue sits just under the 512 KiB line: 400 full checks before the fix, 2 after).
