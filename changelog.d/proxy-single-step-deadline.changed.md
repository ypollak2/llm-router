proxy local path (#508 follow-up): the `fast` trim, the okf attach and the backend now share one step deadline (`t0 + step_budget_s`, as the edit protocol already did). Before, the trim and the backend each got a full `step_budget_s`, so the worst case before falling back to Claude was about 2x the budget plus 2 s (62 s by default, 242 s in local-agent mode). The okf attach keeps its own 2 s cap within the deadline. A step whose budget is spent before the backend call is a `budget_exceeded` fallback, and the backend is not called.
