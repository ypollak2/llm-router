P2.7: user prefixes `local:`, `claude:`, `opus:`, `sonnet:`, `haiku:`, `codex:` (and `native:` as `claude:`) are honoured at three doors. The UserPromptSubmit hook (v60) now honours a Claude-family prefix in every mode, not only zero-Claude, and forces `local:`/`codex:` through the routing directive with the prefix stripped (a prefix beats the classifier, the Q&A-quiet policy and the soft pressure override; enforce off/shadow, kill switches and hard budget caps still win). The proxy pins the named tier (`sonnet:`/`haiku:` generalise the `opus:` pin), strips the prefix from the forwarded body, forces `--serve local-agent` steps local with a one-line refusal instead of falling back to Claude, and records `prefix`/`prefix_outcome` on prefixed ledger rows only. MCP `llm()` runs the named model, or returns a one-line refusal/host note; the budget check and quality breaker still apply. Unknown prefixes are ignored. Each forced choice appends `{text_sha, forced, decided, door, ts}` (no text, bounded to 2 MiB) to `~/.llm-router/examples.jsonl`. Not in this change: parsing in the engine L1 (`engine.py` does not exist yet), and a Codex or serve-off local backend in the proxy (those prefixes are forwarded unchanged with `prefix_outcome=unserved:*`). Evidence: `tests/test_p27_prefixes.py`, `tests/test_p27_proxy_prefixes.py`, `tests/test_p27_hook_prefixes.py` (hook run as a subprocess).
