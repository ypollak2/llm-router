`scripts/release/bind_report.py --since <fix deploy time>` prints the D-358 success bar over a window: Codex binding rate
(the Agent tool_result carries the Codex text and no Claude sub-agent was launched for that tool_use_id), blocked-answer
share among bound units with a stable sample of 20 for the hand check, router holds per 100 organic prompts and fixed-reply
probes (from BIND W2's `hold_events.jsonl` when it has rows, else from the transcripts), the outcome gate under the fixed
audit, and an estimate (labelled) of the Claude output tokens and dollars a bound unit avoids. Each line carries its n, UTC
window, source and PASS, FAIL, INSUFFICIENT or PENDING; the exit code is 0 only when every line passes. The audit gains
`not_applicable` (outside `attempted`, allowlisted signals only), `audit_version`, binding, hold and dedupe counts in its
summary, and `--until` to reproduce a window. `scripts/release/audit_agreement.py` re-runs the audit read-only and reports
agreement with the text-free hand-label fixtures in `tests/fixtures/bind_w3/` (ids are hashes, no prompt or reply text).
