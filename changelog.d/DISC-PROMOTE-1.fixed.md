Models installed at the first run of an upgrade are grandfathered through `grandfathered_models.json` (written once, empty on a fresh install, never recreated if deleted). Behaviour change: a model you pull after upgrading is not preferred until you configure it. `LLM_ROUTER_PROMOTE_GATE=off` restores the previous chains. Evidence: `tests/test_p015_promote_gate.py` and `tests/test_p015_chain_replay.py`.
