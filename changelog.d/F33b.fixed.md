The pinned-model paragraph of docs/empty-chain-behaviour.md now states the exact rule: a pin is dropped only when the irreducible tokens exceed 0.9 times the model's context window, and a pin with an unknown window always passes. It also names the consequence of the untrimmed forward: Ollama's OpenAI-compatible /v1 endpoint can silently drop leading messages when the request exceeds num_ctx. The image helpers `_has_image` and `_newest_user_turn` are now public (`has_image`, `newest_user_turn`) in route_server, and the gateway imports the public names; nothing else imported the old names, so no aliases are kept.
