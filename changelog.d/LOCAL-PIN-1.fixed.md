LOCAL-PIN-1: at the proxy (`--serve local-agent`), a `local:` prompt now forces only that step (and the tool-loop calls that follow it). Before, it also pinned the whole conversation to local, even when the step was refused, so later prompts without a prefix kept going to the local model. Behaviour change: after a `local:` prompt, the next prompt without a prefix is routed as if the prefix had never been used. A conversation pinned to Claude stays on Claude. A conversation the proxy holds no pin for is treated as started elsewhere and goes to Claude. Evidence: `test_local_prefix_serves_one_step_and_the_claude_pin_holds` and `test_a_refused_local_step_leaves_no_local_pin` in `tests/test_p27_proxy_prefixes.py`.
