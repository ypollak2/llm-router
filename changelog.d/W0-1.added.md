W0-1: every routing decision row carries `layer`, `confidence`, `reason` and `inventory_sig`, so "routes to an
ineligible model" and the `llm-router why` layer become countable. New `decision_stamp` module; writers stamped:
`router.route_and_call` (MCP tools and the gateway), the failed-call row in `cost`,
`hooks.savings_logger.log_direct_to_db` (hook, agent-route, SDK) and the proxy ledger (`cls_layer`,
`cls_confidence`, `cls_reason`, `inventory_sig`). `layer` is the engine layer (`L1`/`L3`/`cache`/`fallback`),
else the classifier label (`heuristic`, `llm`, `cached`, `override`, `prefix`, a fast-path name, `rules`, `knn`),
or `unhinted` when the caller named the task. `confidence` is the classifier's own number and NULL when it has
none (override, fallback, unhinted, prefix, 0.0): never fabricated. `inventory_sig` is sha256[:12] of the resource
map's connected set, priority order and policy (not quota or timestamps), cached for 30 s; NULL when there is no
usable map (none, or older than the map reader's 6 h limit). Rows written before this change stay NULL. Not
stamped: the lineage store's separate `routing_decisions` table and `model_tracking.jsonl`.
