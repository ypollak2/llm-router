- Fix the proxy's local backend breaker ignoring a `budget_exceeded` step in which the request was sent and no response headers or stream line came back: it now counts as a failure toward `fail_n` and opens the breaker; a step that never reached the send, one that received headers or lines, and `hedge_timeout` stay neutral. The request counts as sent before the connection pool is acquired and the connection made, so a pool or connect timeout, or a request queued inside the runner that returns no response headers within the step budget, also counts as a failure; a runner that returns nothing within the step budget is the stall being fixed.
