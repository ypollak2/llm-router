`LLM_ROUTER_PIN_SCOPE=turn` (default off, `--serve local-agent`): a failed local step pins only the rest of the current human turn (`cls_decide.turn_idx`, ledger `cls_turn_idx`; reason `pinned_turn`), and local is tried again at the next human turn. After `LLM_ROUTER_PIN_TURN_MAX_FALLBACKS` (default 3) fallbacks in one conversation the pin is sticky under its own reason `pinned_sticky`, which `LLM_ROUTER_CLASSIFIER_AUTHORITY=positive` never overrides. Only this fallback pin changes; kill switch, claude marker, exempt, media and prompt-cap pins are untouched. With the flag on, `local_mode` carries `pin_scope`, `pin_fallbacks` and `pin_cleared`; with it off, no field and no behaviour changes.
