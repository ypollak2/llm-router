Codex `--ignore-rules` capability probe: a definite "no" is no longer cached for the life of the process. It expires after 10 minutes, and any cached answer ("yes" or "no") is dropped as soon as the codex binary's size or modification time changes, so an upgrade is picked up and a downgrade to a codex without the flag is re-checked instead of trusted. If the binary cannot be stat'ed the probe answers "no" and the run path refuses. Review finding N-D of #818.
