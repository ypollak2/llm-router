- Proxy: the classifier SHADOW lane (`proxy/cls_embed_shadow.py`, PR-4), on only with `LLM_ROUTER_CLS_EMBED=shadow` (default off: nothing runs, rows and goldens unchanged). After the rules decide a human turn in local-agent mode, one detached task embeds the assembled prompt on the small Ollama server and appends a text-free record (`cls_emb_*`, `session_id`, `text_sha`, `msg_id`, `pop`, `step_class`, `rules_decision`, `late`) to `cls_embed_shadow.jsonl`. It never changes a routing decision and the request never waits for it: its own 4-thread executor (not the default one), `asyncio.wait_for` at the 250 ms budget (a slow embed is recorded as an abstain, `timeout`, `late: true`), a pending cap of 4 that frees a slot only when the thread ends (overflow is recorded as `queue_full`, never queued), one embed per human turn. Side calls, permission checks (`not_eligible`), `claude_marker` rows, continuations and secret-holding prompts are never embedded. Ledger rows get no new field.
